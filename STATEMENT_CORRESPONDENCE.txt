Verdict
The proof establishes the conjectured polynomial for a precisely completed source
family and nonempty-subfamily closure. The completed row is uniquely forced by
the other printed rows and the printed uniform-frequency assertion. Literal
empty-subfamily closure has one extra member. Author confirmation and acceptance
of the clarified formulation by mathematical/prize reviewers remain external
questions; neither is an axiom used by the unconditional explicit-family theorem.

Parameters and coordinates
The source uses x>=5 in Conjecture 3, n=4x+4. The main theorem proves every natural
x>=3. In Lean q=x+1 and the ground set is Fin(4q); coordinate v means v.val+1 in
the paper's one-based [n]. Row indices are Fin(4q+3), whose values are 0,...,n+2.
No upper bound on x, the number of selected rows, or endpoint values is assumed.

The missing row
The explicit completion is {2,4} union [2q+2,4q-2].
LuRazParametric.exact_frequencies proves its source has exactly 2q+1 occurrences
of every element. LuRazSubmission.sumTo_difference_at_one cancels all unchanged
indicators between two proposed families. Consequently omitted_row_forced and
balanced_completion_unique prove uniqueness of the missing row if the remaining
printed rows and the stated frequency are retained. The result is stronger than
agreement at finitely many x and weaker than an assertion about author intent.
The original OneDrive generator/closure links could not be read in this session;
we do not claim to have compared this definition with the authors' code.

Mathematical family properties
LuRazParametric.completed_for_every_x proves, for all x>=2: complete Reimer
conditions on ordinary subset predicates, nontriviality, frequency 2x+3 for each
element, absence of an abundant element, failure of union closure, all row sizes
at least x and attainment of size x. S_injective proves the rows are distinct.
Thus the parameter x is actually the minimum set size used in the paper.

Closure semantics
LuRazClosure.FinGenerated uses arbitrary nonempty lists of row indices, not a
fixed list-length bound. Duplication of a row does not affect the resulting union.
LuRazSubmission.nonempty_subfamily_iff proves equivalence with arbitrary predicate
selections of nonempty subfamilies of the finite row set. This establishes the
ordinary set-family meaning without assuming a coding-to-sets correspondence.
LuRazFiniteClosure transfers the unbounded natural-coordinate classification to
Fin(4q), proving injectivity for supported predicates and exact membership.

The two closure conventions
The displayed source definition permits an arbitrary subfamily, with no written
nonemptiness condition. Its reported counts x=2,3,4 are 133,233,354. For our
completion these are nonempty-subfamily counts; including the empty subfamily
instead gives 134,234,355. All source rows are nonempty by source_row_nonempty,
so empty_not_generated excludes the empty set from the nonempty closure.
subfamily_union_iff and including_empty_count formally prove the exact +1 change.
We do not relabel this convention discrepancy as a new mathematical refutation.

Counting endpoints
LuRazClosure.closure_cardinality_for_every_x:
 all natural x>=3, nonempty closure count (23x*x+81x+16)/2.
LuRazSubmission.nonempty_count_in_paper_range:
 all natural x>=5, the same result with arbitrary nonempty-subfamily semantics.
LuRazSubmission.nonempty_count_n_scaled:
 32*count=23*n*n+140*n-672, n=4q. Dividing by 32 gives the printed polynomial.
LuRazSubmission.including_empty_count:
 all natural x>=3, arbitrary-subfamily count (23x*x+81x+18)/2.
LuRazSubmission.every_balanced_completion_count:
 the nonempty formula for every completion preserving all printed rows and the
 stated equal frequencies; the completion is not postulated to equal our row.

Evidence and scope exclusions
The Nodup enumeration proves both directions of membership and its exact length.
All fourteen possible cores are covered. The other two core bit patterns are
proved impossible. This is a universal theorem, not interpolation of finite data.
It proves neither global minimality nor Frankl's original union-closed conjecture.
It makes no claim of prize eligibility, first-public priority or official review.
The corresponding mathematical argument is supplied in PROOF.txt.
