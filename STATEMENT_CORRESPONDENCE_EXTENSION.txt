1. Principal original result versus extension result
----------------------------------------------------
ORIGINAL S: The paper's Conjecture 3 uses x>=5, n=4x+4. The formal family has
q=x+1 and source labels 0,...,4q+2. Its explicit completed row at label 2q+1 is
{2,4} union [2q+2,4q-2]. For every natural x>=3, the proved number of distinct
nonempty-subfamily unions is (23x^2+81x+16)/2. In particular the whole paper
range x>=5 is covered. In q-coordinates this is (23q^2+35q-42)/2. The empty-
subfamily convention would instead add exactly one member. These facts establish
the clarified original statement, not a count of the later modified B family.

THREE-ROW V: The first balanced incidence cycle changes original labels 2,4,4q-1.
It has nonempty closure size (23q^2+31q-30)/2 for q>=4. Its source construction,
closure classification and full spectrum are intermediate proved results reused
in the later development. They are not substituted for the original S in the
principal conjecture statement.

SIX-ROW B: Two further balanced cycles modify labels 1,4,4q-1 and then
2q+2,4q-1,4q. The net six changed labels relative to S are
 1, 2, 4, 2q+2, 4q-1, 4q.
B has nonempty closure size (23q^2+7q+2)/2 for q>=4. Its 27 removed and 16 added
groups refer to the difference from C_V, not directly from C_S. Its exact
14-piece spectrum, extrema, parameter thresholds and total weight are extension
claims about this B. A new nomination or a separate scope determination may be
needed; the mathematical proof does not settle that procedural question.

The complete row definitions, the orientations of all three incidence cycles,
the 43 difference forms, and the derivation of the final formulas are given in
PROOF_EXTENSION.txt. Every interval there is inclusive. Row 0 is an actual
source row equal to the whole ground set, not an empty-union label.

2. Coordinates, generation and what is counted
----------------------------------------------
 Ground set             Fin(4q), identified with one-based [1,4q].
 Fin coordinate v       natural coordinate v.val+1, never v.val.
 Source row index i     Fin(4q+3), value 0,...,4q+2, with NO +1 shift.
 BRow q i e             the natural-coordinate incidence predicate.
 B q i v                BRow q i.val (v.val+1).
 Generated q R          a nonempty list of valid row labels has union R.
 FinGenerated q T       the corresponding actual union of finite source B rows.
 ClosureMembers q       an exact, duplicate-free list of resulting sets.
 FinClosureMembers q    its restriction to the actual finite ground set.
 sixRowCardinality q    the ordinary number M of sets in that list, cast to Int.
 sixRowFrequency q e    the ordinary number f(e) of its sets containing e.
 sixRowTotalWeight q    the ordinary total W=sum_T |T|.

Arbitrary row-list length and repeated labels are allowed by Generated; the
union relation has no small-list cutoff. Repetitions do not create additional
sets in the exact Nodup enumeration. Membership and non-membership in the
43 groups are extensional set statements. Their constructor parameter is not
a substitute for the set it evaluates to: semantic injectivity is proved.

The spectrum entry spectrumPolynomial2 is TWICE the ordinary frequency.
The cardinality polynomial 23q^2+7q+2 equals 2M; the weight polynomial
69q^3+15q^2-18q-34 equals 2W. Ratios divide f by M, so the factors of two cancel.
All fourteen spectrum pieces assert 1<=e<=4q and q>=4. The natural-coordinate
frequency is proved zero outside [1,4q]; the polynomial definition has no claimed
frequency interpretation outside that range. All rational inequalities use
cross multiplication by a positive denominator, not rounded percentages.

Source B has 4q+3 rows and each point occurs in 2q+1 of them, below one half.
Closure C_B has M sets, and each point occurs in more than half of them. These
are different families. The package is not a union-closed counterexample with
no abundant element, and supplies no disproof of Frankl's conjecture.

5. What these correspondences do and do not certify
---------------------------------------------------
Every displayed new polynomial is connected to the same actual B generation
relation, using full difference iff proofs, semantic uniqueness, enumeration,
frequency transfers and incidence double counting. No closed formula has been
adopted as a definition of actual closure cardinality, frequency or weight.

The original conjecture, the variant construction problem, and the all-parameter
spectrum problem are distinct claims. All q>=4 extension results are unconditional
mathematical theorems about the explicitly defined B. Their relevance to a
specific prize problem, novelty in the broader literature, independent human
review, authorship/recipient eligibility and reward allocation require separate
review. This document asserts none of those decisions.

The dated manifests and verification.json are the evidence for staged compilation,
axiom audit and bundled-kernel replay. The accompanying narrative deliberately
distinguishes those completed checks from a later all-dependency clean build.
A new consolidated clean-build result, publication commit or official response
must be attached as a new record, not silently read into an older frozen report.
