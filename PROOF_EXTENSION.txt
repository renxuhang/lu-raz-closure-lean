A SIX-ROW MODIFICATION OF THE COMPLETED LU--RAZ FAMILY
Exact union closure, coordinate spectrum, extrema and total weight
English mathematical exposition accompanying the Lean sources
24 October 2026

Scope of this extension
-----------------------
The principal submission remains the exact quadratic count for the explicitly
completed original Lu--Raz construction. This note concerns a different, modified
family. It explains the mathematical content of the subsequent three-row and
six-row research and the proof of the six-row results. It does not assert that a
prize nomination of the original conjecture automatically covers these extensions.

All results below are for integer q >= 4. Put h=2q, n=4q and r=3q+1. Write [a,b]
for the inclusive set of integers a,...,b, taking it to be empty if b<a. All sets
are subsets of U=[1,n]. Row labels are 0,...,n+2; labels are not coordinates.
Let C_X denote all DISTINCT unions of NONEMPTY subfamilies of a source family X.
Its size is M_X. For e in U, f_X(e)=|{T in C_X : e in T}|. Its total weight is
W_X=sum_{T in C_X}|T|. A source row and a member of its closure are different
objects; the source frequency is not the closure frequency.

2. Why B retains the source construction properties
---------------------------------------------------
The common upper filter is indexed by the same row labels:
 F_0=U; F_i=U\{i} for 1<=i<=n;
 F_{n+1}=U\{1,2}; F_{n+2}=U\{3,4}.
It is an upward-closed family of n+3 distinct sets. Every B_i is contained in F_i.
For each pair i!=j, the proof exhibits a coordinate in B_i\F_j or in B_j\F_i.
Consequently the Boolean intervals [B_i,F_i] and [B_j,F_j] are disjoint: a set
belonging to both would have to contain that coordinate and omit it. The same
separating coordinate proves that B_i and B_j are distinct. Thus the full Reimer
condition is witnessed by an actual bijective same-index pairing with this filter,
not just by an average-size inequality or a finite numerical test.

For coatom labels 1,...,n the separation condition is B_i contains j OR B_j
contains i. The unchanged cases inherit the earlier construction's separation;
the pairs involving a changed label are checked from the displayed six rows.
For the two extra filter members, one uses coordinates 1,2 and 3,4 respectively;
row 0 is separated from a coatom by the coatom's missing coordinate. These cases
exhaust all distinct labels and also establish row injectivity.

Each incidence triple has one deletion and one addition in every affected row,
and one deletion and one addition in every affected column. Summing indicators
therefore preserves every original row size and every column frequency. Hence
B has n+3 distinct rows; its minimum row size is q-1, attained at row n+1; and each
coordinate occurs in exactly 2q+1 source rows. Since 2(2q+1)=4q+2<4q+3, B has
no element in at least half its source rows.

B itself is not union closed. Rows n+1 and n+2 have a union containing 1 and n
but omitting 3. Every source row containing both 1 and n contains 3, as follows
by its row case. Their union is therefore not a source row. Its closure C_B is,
of course, union closed; later bounds show that EVERY coordinate occurs in more
than half the members of C_B. There is no contradiction between these statements.

4. Why the two difference tables are exhaustive
------------------------------------------------
The proof does not infer these tables by fitting finite closures. Here is the
structural reduction used to prove their universal set meaning.

Generation criterion. A set T is in C_B if and only if (a) some source row lies
inside T and (b) each point e of T is covered by a source row lying inside T.
The forward implication takes a row of the generating subfamily containing e.
For the reverse implication, take all source rows contained in T. There are
only n+3 candidate rows; (a) makes the collection nonempty, and (b) makes its
union exactly T. This supplies both generation witnesses and non-generation
obstructions without bounding the size of a union expression.

Core restriction. Once K=T intersect {1,2,3,4} is fixed, no row whose small-core
contains a point outside K may be selected. Checking the row definition therefore
restricts all possible generators before their tails are combined. There are
16 core masks. Masks 0,1,9 have unchanged closures, and mask 8 cannot occur.
For the remaining masks the restricted row tails are intervals with at most a
small fixed collection of isolated endpoints. Unions of the J-type rows are
recorded by their leftmost and rightmost interval endpoints. Unions of L-type
rows are recorded by their minimum suffix parameter and maximum prefix
parameter. The exceptional L-row at parameter q+3 is kept separately because
it omits n. All fixed rows are selected by Boolean flags. An induction over
an arbitrary list of selected rows proves these reductions, rather than just
reducing lists of some preselected maximum length.

A typical J union has core {1,4} and tail [a,b], where
 5<=a<=h+1, h+2<=b<=n-3, and g(a)<=b,
 g(a)=h+a-3 for a<=q+3, and g(a)=h+a-4 otherwise.
These inequalities characterize reachable endpoint pairs and have converse row
witnesses. Adding fixed prefixes or suffixes either absorbs an endpoint or
leaves a visible gap; this yields a finite list of canonical possibilities.

For example, in core 13={1,3,4}, every B-generated set has one of these eight
shapes, always with K_13 adjoined:
 [5,n];
 [5,b],                           h+2<=b<=n-3;
 [5,b] union {n-1},               r<=b<=n-3;
 [5,b] union {r,n-1},             h+2<=b<=3q-1;
 [5,b] union {n-1,n},             h+2<=b<=n-3;
 [5,b] union {r,n-1,n},           h+2<=b<=3q-1;
 [5,b] union [3q+2,n],            h+2<=b<=3q;
 [5,b] union [r,n],               h+2<=b<=3q-1.
Indeed the fixed row n now supplies C1 rather than C, the fixed row n-1
supplies D rather than L, and the row 2 supplies Z. With a J interval present,
the other optional fixed rows reduce to these endpoint flags. Core point 4
forces at least one J row: none of the allowed fixed rows supplies that point.
Core point 3 in turn forces at least one of rows n, n-1 or 2. Conversely all
eight shapes have explicit source-row unions. Comparing this list with the
proved V classification gives exactly the four removed and three added core-13
groups in Section 3, including the shortened parameter ranges when endpoints
are absorbed. One instructive obstruction is r13cj(b): for b<=r-2 its point r
is isolated. A row needed to supply that isolated point in B also forces n-1,
which this set lacks. A J interval cannot supply r without filling the missing
intermediate coordinate. The old set is consequently not B-generated.

Core 11={1,2,4} illustrates the other endpoint direction. A J interval together
with at least one of rows 1,h+1,h+2 becomes a right interval [a,n-2], optionally
with {n} or {n-1,n}. Adding row n+2 either is absorbed or leaves the visible
prefix A. There are also five fixed-row unions with A; two further apparent
fixed choices are already the gapped shape at a=h+1. Reducing them prevents
counting those sets twice. This is the source of r11j/r11aj/r11ak and their
three added counterparts.

In core 6={2,3}, the changed singleton/fixed part combines with the original
L-interval unions. Prefix/suffix absorption leaves the retained two-parameter
L shapes, one filled exceptional shape, the regular family C0 union Suf(t),
the exceptional suffix ending at n-1, and [5,p] union [h+3,n]. Core 7 also has
the optional core-1 rows. Taking their unions with this core-6 list, then
normalizing prefix absorption, yields the retained V shapes plus the four
added groups in the core-7 table. These are genuine two-way classifications:
valid new normal forms are generated, and every actual union has such a form.

For core 14={2,3,4}, the old core-234 normal forms suffice after the relevant
endpoint cases are removed: the changed rows can be generated within the old
core classification, while the surviving old normal forms have B row witnesses.
Thus this core has removed groups and no added group. The small cores 2,3,4,5,
10,12 reduce directly to unions of a fixed finite collection of possible rows.
Across all cores avoiding 2 and 4 there are 16 canonical fixed-row unions.
The cases with core 5 give the seven new and one lost tails in the tables.

For full core 15 the proof first shows that every B union belongs to the old
full-core classification. It then proves and uses additional covering tests:
 (q+2 in T OR h+1 in T),
 (h+2 in T OR h+3 in T),
 (h+2 in T OR n in T),
together with the already established V tests. Necessity follows from rows
covering core point 1, core point 4, and the relevant endpoint coordinates.
Sufficiency is proved by constructing unions for every surviving old full-core
normal form. Filtering the old forms by these tests removes exactly the five
core-15 groups and adds none. For example, if h+2 is absent, a row covering 4
cannot be row h+1; the possible remaining source rows force both h+3 and n.
This explains why a member containing 4 but lacking h+2 and n cannot survive.

Each core comparison is an iff, not just a list of observed differences.
Combining the 16 disjoint cases proves, for every ordinary subset T of U,
 C_B contains T iff (C_V contains T and T is in no removed group)
                    OR T is in an added group.
It also proves that the removed table is exactly C_V\C_B and the added table
is exactly C_B\C_V. These statements are removed_exact, added_exact and
closure_delta_exact in SixRowGlobalDifference.lean. No unproved classification
hypothesis is an input to them.

5. Uniqueness, typed enumeration and the cardinality formula
-----------------------------------------------------------
Distinct core masks give different sets. Within one core, an interval endpoint
is recovered from the membership of the endpoint and its next point; isolated
points n-1,n,r and the first point of a gap distinguish the shape constructors.
For the regular suffix groups beta is strictly increasing on G: its images are
 [h+4,3q+1] and [3q+3,n-1].
Its argument is recovered by testing a point present at the smaller suffix
start but absent at the larger start and not already supplied by C or C0. If
the smaller start is r (which the fixed tail already contains), use r+1 instead;
the next admissible suffix starts at r+2. The exception ending at n-1 is
distinguished from a regular suffix by n. Short-prefix sentinels and filled
cases are distinguished by a missing middle coordinate. For a two-parameter
r15short set, the end of the low prefix recovers p (p=4 means no low prefix),
and membership of h+2 distinguishes the two possible t values.

For the finite selectors r12 and n5, a sufficient ordered collection of tests
is h+2,r,3q+2,n-1,n. In particular the seven n5 tails have these distinct
membership signatures, in selector order k=0,...,6:
 k=0: (1,0,0,0,0)     k=1: (0,1,0,1,0)     k=2: (1,1,0,1,0)
 k=3: (1,0,0,1,1)     k=4: (0,1,0,1,1)     k=5: (1,0,1,1,1)
 k=6: (0,1,1,1,1).
The coordinate 3q+2 is essential in this collection: it distinguishes D union Z
from D union H, and C1 union Z from C1 union H. The r12 tails L, L union H,
L union C, L union H union C likewise have distinct signatures under these
tests. These calculations hold throughout q>=4 because h+2<r<3q+2<n-1<n.
The proof also checks all cross-constructor cases, not only injectivity within
a constructor. Consequently equality of two valid evaluated
removed forms forces equality of their complete typed forms; likewise for added
forms. This rules out duplicate sets both within and between the displayed groups.

The typed lists are constructed by the indicated inclusive integer ranges, not
by evaluating all subsets and discarding duplicates. Their membership iff Valid
and their typed Nodup properties are proved. Evaluation is injective on those
valid forms by the preceding argument, so the lists of actual sets are Nodup.

The natural-coordinate enumeration is supported in [1,n]. Restriction to Fin(n)
is injective on supported sets, and lifting an actual finite union recovers its
supported natural-coordinate set. Thus exactness and Nodup transfer to the
ordinary finite source B, not to a separate counting model. The source list may
repeat row labels when representing a union, but the enumeration counts each
resulting SET once. Since all source rows are nonempty, the empty set is absent;
allowing the empty subfamily would add exactly one empty set. Its frequency and
weight contributions would be zero, but its additional member would change all
frequency ratios. The ratios below always use nonempty closure.

6. Counting incidences in the difference groups
----------------------------------------------
For any duplicate-free set list L define CountAt(L,e) as the number of its sets
containing e. The two actual list decompositions above give the pointwise identity
 f_B(e)=f_V(e)-CountAt(R,e)+CountAt(N,e).
This holds for all natural-coordinate e, including e outside U, where the
frequencies vanish. Closed polynomial formulas below are asserted only on U.

The individual group counts are exact integer interval counts. Put
 LE(a,b,x)=max(0,min(b,x)-a+1),
 GE(a,b,x)=max(0,b-max(a,x)+1).
For a<=b these count parameters t in [a,b] satisfying t<=x or x<=t respectively.
Use 1[P] for the 0-or-1 indicator of a proposition P. A fixed core point appears
in every set of its group. For e>=5, its occurrences are obtained by testing the
tail. A fixed tail absorbs an optional interval; its contribution is therefore
counted once, rather than added twice where two pieces overlap.

For example, the r11j group has 2q-3 members. At a tail coordinate e its count is
 1[e<=n-1] LE(5,h+1,e).
Its new counterpart n11j has tail count
 1[e<=n-2] LE(5,h+1,e) + (2q-3) 1[e=n].
For a regular C-union-suffix group, if e is in the fixed C tail, every one of its
2q-5 members contains e. Otherwise its suffix contribution is
 LE(h+4,3q+1,e)+LE(3q+3,n-1,e),
for 5<=e<=n. These are precisely the two images of beta on G. This argument
requires the omitted suffix value 3q+2; it does not replace beta by an unbroken
interval. For the r15short group, the tail count is
 2 GE(4,q+1,e) 1[5<=e<=q+1]
   +(q-2)(1[h+2<=e<=n]+1[h+3<=e<=n]).
For n7right, the t=0 exceptional tail ends at n-1. It is counted separately
from the ordinary t interval, so the count at n is q-2, not q-1.

The same elementary procedure is proved for all 43 groups. CountAt is additive
over list append, so their counts sum to the removed and added frequencies.
The definitions and proofs in SixRowDifferenceCountsDefs.lean,
SixRowFiniteGroupCounts.lean, the Removed/Added/Regular/SpecialGroupCounts
modules, and SixRowDifferenceCounts.lean implement these reductions. The
polynomials are derived from actual list counts; they are not new definitions
of what "frequency" is supposed to mean.

8. Exact extrema, exceptional parameter, and rational thresholds
----------------------------------------------------------------
Write D_q=23q^2+7q+2=2M_B,
 A_q=15q^2+7q-14, and Z_q=19q^2+q+2.
Comparing each of the fourteen polynomials with A_q and Z_q, using its inclusive
endpoint bounds, proves
 min_e f_B(e)=A_q/2, with equality exactly at e=3q;
 max_e f_B(e)=Z_q/2.
For q>=5 the maximum is attained exactly at e=q+2. For q=4 it is attained
exactly at e=6 and e=15, both with value 155. The minimum is still unique:
e=12, with value 127. In particular, uniqueness of the maximum holds iff q>=5.
Also f_B(q+2)=f_B(1)+1.

The comparisons can be checked directly by endpoint differences and concavity
of the quadratic pieces; all coordinate cells are separately certified in
SixRowSpectrumExtremaCells.lean. One useful explanation of the exceptional tie
is Z_q-(18q^2+6q-2)=(q-1)(q-4), so the endpoint n-1 ties the usual maximizer only
at q=4. The core-1 coordinate never ties: its doubled value is exactly Z_q-2.

Since M_B>0, abundance ratios can be compared by integer cross multiplication.
The minimum formula and the factorization
 199 A_q-127 D_q=8(q-4)(8q+95)
prove the sharp uniform lower bound f_B(e)/M_B>=127/199. Equality forces q=4
and then the unique minimum e=12. That actual set-family example also proves
that no larger rational constant works uniformly for all q>=4 and all e.
At the other end,
 19 D_q-23 Z_q=110q-8>0
proves f_B(e)/M_B<19/23 for every q and coordinate. In particular all coordinates
are abundant, strictly above one half, in the closure.

For the stronger threshold 15/23, the exact minimum comparison is
 23 A_q-15 D_q=8(7q-44).
For integer q>=4 this is positive exactly when q>=7. It follows that
 (every coordinate e has f_B(e)>15 M_B/23) iff q>=7.
Failure at q=4,5,6 is witnessed by e=3q, not inferred from a few sampled values.
The ratios A_q/D_q and Z_q/D_q tend algebraically to 15/23 and 19/23. The formal
results express sharpness without a real-analysis limit: for any rational
constant above 15/23 and any starting Q>=4, some integer q>=Q has its minimum
ratio below that constant; for any nonnegative rational constant below 19/23,
some q>=Q has its maximum ratio above it. These are tail-sharp statements, not
a claim that 15/23 is a uniform lower bound starting already at q=4.

Finally, among INTEGER parameters of this particular B family, the minimum
ratio A_q/D_q is maximized at q=13, with value 653/995. Indeed
 653 D_q-995 A_q=(q-13)(94q-1172).
For q<=12 both factors are negative; for q>=14 both are positive; only q=13
gives zero. This establishes both the bound and unique parameter, not optimality
among all Reimer constructions or all union-closed families.

9. Total weight and average size
--------------------------------
Counting the incidence pairs (T,e) in the two orders gives
 W_B=sum_{T in C_B}|T|=sum_{e=1}^{4q} f_B(e).
The formal double-counting lemma is applied to the same exact closure list as
in Sections 5--7. Each of the fourteen consecutive coordinate ranges is summed
using the integer formulas for sums of 1,e,e^2. The result is
 2 W_B=69q^3+15q^2-18q-34.
The sum is of the DOUBLED spectrum, so division by two produces the ordinary
weight; there is no extra factor of two in W_B's definition. The same identity
holds for the actual finite subsets of Fin(4q), with sizes counting all 4q
coordinates once. Combining it with the cardinality gives
 3q M_B-W_B=3q^2+12q+17>0.
Thus the average member size is strictly below 3q. Using the old V weight,
 W_V-W_B=35q^2-35q-26.
At q=4 these statements give M_B=199 and W_B=2275.
